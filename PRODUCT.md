# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (React-based framework) - chosen for its excellent performance, built-in routing, image optimization, and deployment flexibility, ideal for a portfolio that needs to load fast and look impeccable across devices.

## Users

Portfolio/showcase visitors: potential clients, employers, collaborators, or peers who are reviewing the designer's work to evaluate capabilities, aesthetic sensibility, and creative approach. They arrive with varying levels of intent—from casual browsing to serious evaluation—and need to quickly understand the quality and range of work.

## Product Purpose

A portfolio website that showcases design work in a way where the portfolio itself serves as a creative statement. Rather than being a neutral container for work samples, the site demonstrates design thinking, craft, and personality through its own execution. Success means visitors remember both the work shown AND the experience of viewing it.

## Positioning

Portfolio as performance: the site itself is proof of design capability. Where standard templates treat the portfolio as infrastructure, this treats it as artifact—the interface, interactions, and presentation style are deliberate creative choices that reinforce the work they contain.

## Operating Context

Visitors typically:
- Arrive from social media, job applications, or referrals
- View on various devices (mobile browsing is common)
- Spend 30 seconds to 5 minutes depending on interest level
- May be comparing multiple portfolios
- Look for both finished work and evidence of process/thinking

The portfolio competes for attention in an environment where many designers use similar templates or platforms (Behance, Dribbble, Webflow templates).

## Capabilities and Constraints

**Must have:**
- Project gallery/showcase as primary content
- Individual project detail pages or expanded views
- About/bio section establishing designer identity
- Contact mechanism
- Fast load times (portfolio visitors have low patience for slow sites)
- Excellent mobile experience

**Deliberately undecided:**
- Specific projects to feature (will use placeholder content initially)
- Whether to include writing/blog content
- Social proof (testimonials, client logos) - depends on what evidence exists
- Case study depth per project

## Brand Commitments

None established yet. The portfolio's visual identity and voice remain to be defined through the design process.

## Evidence on Hand

No existing content, assets, or testimonials specified. The implementation will need to accommodate:
- Project imagery (screenshots, mockups, photos)
- Project descriptions and metadata
- Designer bio and contact information

**Absences:** Do not fabricate client names, testimonials, project details, or claims about impact/results without real examples from the user.

## Product Principles

1. **The container is content** - Every design choice in the portfolio interface itself demonstrates capability; craft is proven through execution, not claimed in copy.

2. **Immediate impact, progressive depth** - Hook attention in the first viewport, then reward exploration with layers of detail for those who stay.

3. **Speed is respect** - Performance is a design quality; visitors judging design work will absolutely judge a slow-loading portfolio.

4. **Work-first hierarchy** - The design work being showcased is always the hero; interface elements support and enhance but never compete with the portfolio pieces.

5. **Memorable through motion and detail** - Thoughtful micro-interactions, transitions, and polish separate a creative statement from a functional container.
